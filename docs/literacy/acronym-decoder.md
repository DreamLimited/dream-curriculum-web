# Literacy — Acronym Decoder

*One-line cheat sheet for the acronyms of this discipline. When you see an acronym you do not recognize, check here first — then check the glossary for the full story.*

| Acronym | Stands for | One-line meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **8(a)** | (Section 8(a) of the Small Business Act) | SBA's business-development program for socially and economically disadvantaged small businesses. |
| **BAA** | Broad Agency Announcement | A research solicitation that invites ideas rather than one specific product. |
| **BOE** | Basis of Estimate | The written justification behind a cost number in a price. |
| **BPA** | Blanket Purchase Agreement | A standing agreement under which agencies place repeated orders. |
| **CAGE** | Commercial and Government Entity | Your 5-character DoD/NATO identity code. |
| **CDRL** | Contract Data Requirements List | The schedule of data deliverables (reports, manuals) a contract requires. |
| **CPARS** | Contractor Performance Assessment Reporting System | The federal registry of how you actually performed on past contracts. |
| **DD-254** | DoD Contract Security Classification Specification | The form that passes security-classification rules to contractors. |
| **DFARS** | Defense Federal Acquisition Regulation Supplement | The DoD rulebook layered on top of the FAR. |
| **FAR** | Federal Acquisition Regulation | The rulebook for how the government buys things. |
| **FPDS** | Federal Procurement Data System | Where contract award data lives (awards are published here). |
| **GSA** | General Services Administration | The agency that runs shared procurement vehicles (Schedules, and more). |
| **HUBZone** | Historically Underutilized Business Zone | An SBA program giving preferences to businesses in designated areas. |
| **IDIQ** | Indefinite-Delivery / Indefinite-Quantity | A standing contract you win once; the agency places orders against it. |
| **JV** | Joint Venture | Two or more firms pursuing a contract as one team or entity. |
| **LPTA** | Lowest Price Technically Acceptable | An evaluation where price decides it, once the technical bar is passed. |
| **MAS** | Multiple Award Schedule | The modern name for the GSA Schedule program. |
| **NAICS** | North American Industry Classification System | The 6-digit code that says what industry you are in. |
| **NOFO** | Notice of Funding Opportunity | The grants-world announcement of a funding competition. |
| **OCI** | Organizational Conflict of Interest | A bias risk when a firm's other work taints impartiality or gives unfair advantage. |
| **OMB** | Office of Management and Budget | The executive office that shapes the federal budget and grants policy. |
| **OTA** | Other Transaction Agreement | A fast, flexible agreement shape for prototypes and research. |
| **pWin** | Probability of Win | Your disciplined estimate of how likely you are to win a pursuit. |
| **PWS** | Performance Work Statement | An outcome-focused description of the work in a performance-based contract. |
| **R&D** | Research and Development | The work that SBIR/STTR fund. |
| **RFI** | Request for Information | The agency is shopping; no award yet. |
| **RFP** | Request for Proposal | The full competition document for a contract buy. |
| **RFQ** | Request for Quote | A pricing request, usually for simpler buys. |
| **SAM** | System for Award Management | The master federal registry; you register here to receive federal money. |
| **SBA** | Small Business Administration | The agency that runs small-business programs and certifications. |
| **SBIR** | Small Business Innovation Research | A phased R&D program for small businesses. |
| **SOW** | Statement of Work | The description of the actual work in a contract solicitation. |
| **STTR** | Small Business Technology Transfer | An R&D program that requires a research-institution partner. |
| **UEI** | Unique Entity ID | Your 12-character identity in federal systems (replaced DUNS). |
| **WOSB** | Woman-Owned Small Business | A small-business certification with set-aside preferences. |
| **VOSB** | Veteran-Owned Small Business | A small-business certification with set-aside preferences. |

## The unofficial truth

Acronyms are how this discipline compresses experience into a word. Do not try to memorize all of them at once. Read the doctrine and the modules, and keep this page open; the acronyms will become familiar the way the letters of a keyboard become familiar — without you noticing the learning.
