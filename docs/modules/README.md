# The Module Ladder — Undergraduate, Graduate, Doctoral, Forever-Learning

*The teaching-plan layer of the Dream Pursuit Doctrine curriculum. This page is the index that links the three degree-tier folders and the shared core, so a learner or instructor can see the ladder at a glance.*

The repository has **two kinds of teaching plans**:

1. **The shared core and the audience tracks** (single-semester, graduate-altitude): `shared-core.md` is the six-session spine; `strategic-initiative.md`, `mba.md`, and `mpa.md` are the three audience tracks used by the 14-week semester course ([`course/syllabus-semester.md`](../course/syllabus-semester.md)). Two cross-cutting modules sit alongside the tracks: [`human-ai-collaboration.md`](human-ai-collaboration.md) (running the gate with a machine — brief, review, hold accountable) and [`company-building.md`](company-building.md) (standing a GovCon firm up from zero, for MBA and Strategic Initiative students).
2. **The degree-tier module folders** (full programs): `undergraduate/`, `graduate/`, `doctoral/`, and `forever-learning/` carry the teaching plans for the four-rung degree ladder. Every module still follows the contract in [`modules/module-template.md`](module-template.md).

## How to read the ladder

- **Start with the shared core.** Every tier teaches the same durable doctrine — `shared-core.md` is the fastest way to see the spine.
- **Undergraduate spreads it thin.** The undergrad folder (`modules/undergraduate/README.md`) explains how the shared six-session arc becomes a fourteen-week undergraduate rhythm with weekly deliverables.
- **Graduate deepens it.** The graduate modules (`modules/graduate/`) re-teach the same concepts at full working-professional depth — finance, game theory, research methods — and prepare the student for the doctorate. The v0.5.0 electives add [pipeline economics & portfolio management](graduate/pipeline-economics.md) (GE 650) and [orals coaching & presentation discipline](graduate/elective-orals-coaching.md) (GE 651).
- **Doctorate makes it a research field.** The doctoral tier (`modules/doctoral/`) is not more depth on the same material; it is the discipline turned into research questions, study designs, and evidence. The [ORBITAL Outcome-Data Research Agenda](doctoral/research-agenda.md) is the map of open questions the doctorate exists to answer.
- **Forever-Learning sustains it.** The forever-learning tier (`modules/forever-learning/`) is the fourth rung: the standing programs — [executive education](forever-learning/executive-education.md), the [post-doctoral fellowship](forever-learning/post-doctoral-fellowship.md), the [alumni community](forever-learning/alumni-community.md), the [refresh and recertification machinery](forever-learning/refresh-and-recertification.md), and the [twenty-year arc](forever-learning/twenty-year-arc.md) — that keep a person learning, teaching, and contributing for a career. Its program overview is [course/forever-learning-program.md](../course/forever-learning-program.md).

## Keeping this index honest

When you add a module to any tier folder, add it to the manifest ([`manifest.yaml`](../manifest.yaml)) in the same change, per the repo's editing rules ([`CLAUDE.md`](../CLAUDE.md)). If the ladder itself changes (a new tier, a renamed program), update this page and the crosswalk in [`align/concept-to-system-map.md`](../align/concept-to-system-map.md) together.
